Floyd Enterprise Ltd privacy notice

Last updated 21 October 2026.

Who we are

Floyd Enterprise Ltd manages your tenancy and is responsible for your information (the "controller"). Contact us at floydenterpriseltd@gmail.com. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, registration number ZC278834.

What we use and why

We use your name, contact details, what you tell us in emails and practical details about your home to run your tenancy: answering questions, arranging repairs and keeping a record of what was agreed. We do this because it's needed for your tenancy agreement, and because we have a legitimate interest in running our properties well.

If you tell us about your health, we use it only to deal with that request (for example, arranging access for a repair). We don't keep it in our notes or pass it on.

Who we share it with

  • Our email provider holds our mailbox.
  • An AI service reads tenants' emails to help us summarise them and prepare replies. A person checks and sends every reply; nothing is sent automatically.
  • An encrypted backup service in the UK.
  • Tradespeople get only what they need for a repair, such as your first name, phone number and access arrangements.

Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Outside the UK

Our email provider and AI service are in the USA. Your information is protected there by the UK's approved contract terms for international transfers (the UK Addendum to the standard contractual clauses).

How long we keep it

  • We keep emails as the record of your tenancy for 6 years after it ends, then delete them.
  • Our working notes are deleted 90 days after a matter is finished, or a year after they were last needed. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 6 months.
  • Records that we gave you information the law requires (dates only) are kept for 6 years after your tenancy ends.
  • Copies of the safety and deposit certificates for your home, which may show your name, are kept for 6 years after a newer one replaces them.
  • Our AI service deletes what it reads within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, ask us to limit how we use it, or object to how we use it. Email floydenterpriseltd@gmail.com; we'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
