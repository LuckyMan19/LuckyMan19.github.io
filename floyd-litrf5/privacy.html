Floyd Enterprise Ltd privacy notice

Last updated 24 October 2026.

Who we are

Floyd Enterprise Ltd is the controller of your information. Contact: floydenterpriseltd@gmail.com. ICO registration ZC278834.

What we hold

Your name and contact details, tenancy and rent records, details about your home, and what you send us (emails, messages, photos), including any health or other sensitive details you choose to share.

Why

To manage your tenancy and our properties: repairs, safety checks, rent and deposits, and our legal duties as a landlord. Legal basis: your tenancy agreement, our legal obligations, and our legitimate interest in managing our properties and tenancies, and protecting our legal position. We need the details your tenancy requires; without them we can't manage it.

Who we share it with

Providers who help run the business (email, IT and software, backups), tradespeople, letting agents, deposit schemes, utility companies, councils, our advisers, and anyone the law requires. Some providers are in the USA, covered by the UK–US data bridge or UK-approved contract terms; email us for a copy of the terms.

How long we keep it

While you're our tenant and for 6 years after. Safety certificates: 6 years after a newer one replaces them. Other information is deleted when it's no longer needed.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct, delete or limit it, object to its use, or ask for a copy you can move to someone else. Email floydenterpriseltd@gmail.com. You can complain to us first (email above), or to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
