Floyd Enterprise Ltd privacy notice

Last updated 22 October 2026.

Who we are

Floyd Enterprise Ltd is the controller of your information. Contact: floydenterpriseltd@gmail.com. ICO registration ZC278834.

What we use and why

Your name, contact details, your emails and details about your home, to manage your tenancy. Legal basis: performing your tenancy agreement, and our legitimate interest in managing our properties. Health information you give us is used only to deal with that request.

Who we share it with

Our email provider, an AI service that helps prepare replies (a person approves each one), an encrypted backup service, and tradespeople (only what a repair needs). Our email provider and AI service are in the USA, covered by the UK Addendum to the standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Emails: 6 years after your tenancy ends.
  • Working notes: 90 days after a matter is finished, or a year after last use. Backups: up to 6 months.
  • Records of information we gave you: 6 years after your tenancy ends.
  • Certificates for your home: 6 years after a newer one replaces them.
  • We keep repair records for 6 years after each repair is closed, then remove your name.
  • Our AI service deletes what it reads within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct, delete or limit it, or object to its use. Email floydenterpriseltd@gmail.com. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk, 0303 123 1113.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
