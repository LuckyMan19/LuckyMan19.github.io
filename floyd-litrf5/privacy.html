Floyd privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

Floyd is a private tool used only by Floyd Enterprise Ltd. It connects to one Gmail account: the company's own.

What Floyd reads

Floyd reads emails from the company's tenants and contractors so it can summarise each one and draft replies for staff to check. For any other email it only looks at the sender and subject, and does not read or share it.

Who it's shared with

To write summaries and drafts, the text of these emails is sent to Anthropic, which provides the AI model Floyd uses. Nothing is shared with anyone else, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Nothing is sent automatically

Floyd only creates drafts. A person reads and sends every email themselves.

What Floyd keeps

Floyd does not keep copies of emails; they stay in Gmail. It keeps short notes and drafts, encrypted, and deletes finished items after 90 days. Encrypted backups are kept for up to 6 months.

Google user data

Floyd's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
